## Slide 2: The Core Problem & Forensic Reality
### The Three Inefficiencies Costing India's Youth
1. **The Annual Allocation Inertia:** District seat targets are incremented by 5-10% from historical baselines without evaluating factory closures, local capex, or industrial shifts.
2. **The "Seat = Worker" Fallacy:** 1,000 sanctioned seats $\neq$ 1,000 ready workers. Dropouts (15%), exam failures (12%), and out-migration (25%) drain the effective local talent pool.
3. **The White-Collar Web Bias:** Scraped job portals represent Tier-1 urban IT/services (NSQF 6-9), leaving vocational trades (NSQF 2-5: welders, solar technicians, EV diagnostics) completely unmeasured.

## Slide 4: Key Innovations & Competitive Differentiators
1. **Mathematical Rigor:**
   - **Composite Demand Index (CDI):** Fuses volume (35%), momentum (25%), employer breadth (15%), skill dispersion (15%), and PLFS macro indicators (10%).
   - **Minimum Trace (MinT) Spatial Reconciliation:** Mathematically ensures district forecasts sum coherently to state and national sector totals.
   - **Split Conformal Prediction:** 80% & 95% distribution-free confidence bounds without assuming Gaussian errors.
2. **Interactive Policy Simulation Sandbox:**
   - Evaluates seat shifts, batch additions, course conversions, and fiscal impacts in **$< 20$ milliseconds**.
   - Generates prescriptive District Skill Development Plan (DSDP) seat allocation recommendations.
3. **Inclusive, Bilingual, Accessible Experience:**
   - Native toggle between **English and Hindi (हिन्दी)**.
   - High-contrast WCAG 2.1 AA compliant UI, dual-metric visualizations, and interactive India SVG choropleth map.
